Discussion Topic #1

"I wonder if you feel locked up or taken captive. If so, have you considered that you might be trapped in a self-made prison?"

  • Sometimes it's easier to have negative self-talk as a man.
  • It's a really good thing that God didn't care about Paul's past and focused on his plan for Paul's future and God's calling in our own lives.
  • Having big dreams/aspirations for what I want to accomplish — when something doesn't go my way it definitely gets me down. Important to celebrate the little victories and small gains to keep moving forward and get out of a pessimistic mindset.

Discussion Topic #2

"Satan is your enemy, and every day he is prowling around (1 Peter 5:8), watching you, looking for a place where you might believe a lie."

  • Satan doesn't use completely unbelievable lies — they're based on our weaknesses/shortcomings.
  • You have to be brave in asking for help / going to seek God and peace based on your circumstances.
  • You have to be searching for the gaps/cracks in the armor where you could get tagged with a lie, and identify it so you can find the fix.

Discussion Question #1

What's a lie you've told yourself that was limiting to your goals or your faith walk?

  • I've lied to myself about how I'm living my life — I've felt invincible, or that I was super into my faith walk when I wasn't in fact living how I should be. Letting ego get in the way and cloud my judgement (something bad/unfortunate happening typically clears that up).
  • In the workplace, I have to do things other people's way and feel like I might not have good ideas — stop limiting myself by putting my own capabilities in a box and trusting myself.
  • "All believers" have some sins that they habitually commit and have attributed to "being alright" — adjusting the standards for themselves and how they view sin as a whole. Then other sins (the ones they don't struggle with) are still upheld as being awful, deplorable things, and through that lens they condemn and judge others. It's religion/faith based on convenience, not on truth.
