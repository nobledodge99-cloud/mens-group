Replace the Lie

A simple tool for tearing down strongholds: remove the lie, replace it with truth, and declare it.

How it works

  1. Name the lie. Be specific: the exact thought, in your own words, as it shows up in real life.
  2. Find the truth. Pick a verse that directly answers that lie. Not a general verse; one that contradicts it.
  3. Declare it. Write a short first-person statement that says what God says about you, and say it out loud every time the lie shows up.

Based on the "Replacement Principle" in Winning the War in Your Mind by Craig Groeschel (Part 1).

The weapons (why this works)

If the enemy's main weapon is lies, our greatest counterweapon is the truth of God's Word, and not just reading it, but wielding it. These are the verses behind the method (page 45).

Hebrews 4:12God's Word is alive, active, and sharper than any sword
For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

Use it for: reminding yourself that Scripture can cut through the thoughts you can't untangle on your own.

Jeremiah 17:9The problem: our own heart can mislead us
The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?

Use it for: being skeptical of your own strongholds. A thought feeling true doesn't make it true.

Ephesians 6:17The Word of God is the sword of the Spirit
And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:

Use it for: treating your chosen verse as a weapon you pick up on purpose, not background reading.

Romans 12:2Transformation happens through a renewed mind
And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Use it for: remembering that God is the one who renews the mind. Your part is to show up with the truth and keep at it.

2 Timothy 2:25-26Truth is how we escape the devil's trap
25In meekness instructing those that oppose themselves; if God peradventure will give them repentance to the acknowledging of the truth; 26And that they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will.

Use it for: seeing the stakes: lies are a trap, and knowing the truth is the way out.

John 8:32The truth sets you free
And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.

Use it for: the promise at the end of the process. This is what the whole exercise is aimed at.

2 Corinthians 10:5Take every thought captive (from our own notes)
Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

Use it for: the action step. When the lie shows up, you stop it and run it through the template.

The template

Lie"[The exact thought, in first person, the way it sounds in your head.]"
Truth"[A verse that directly contradicts the lie.]" ([Book Chapter:Verse])
Declaration"God tells me [what's true about me or about Him]. [One or two short sentences in your own words.]"

Tips for filling it out

  1. Write the lie as "I", not "people say." If you wouldn't actually think it, it's not the real one.
  2. The verse has to answer the lie directly. If you can't connect them in one sentence, pick another.
  3. Keep the declaration short enough to say out loud in about 10 seconds.
  4. Start the declaration with "God tells me…" so it's clear whose words you're standing on.
  5. Revisit it with your accountability partner.

Examples

Example 1: Self-reliance

Lie

"I can handle this on my own. I don't need anyone's help."

Truth

"Two are better than one… For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up."

Ecclesiastes 4:9-10
Declaration

"God tells me I wasn't built to fight alone. I bring my struggles to Him and to my brothers, and that's strength, not weakness."

Example 2: Not good enough

Lie

"I'm not good enough for what God has called me to."

Truth

"I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me."

Philippians 4:13
Declaration

"God tells me my strength comes from Him, not from me. I don't have to be enough. He is."

Example 3: Sin too big

Lie

"What I've done is too big. God is done with me."

Truth

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

1 John 1:9
Declaration

"God tells me I'm forgiven and cleansed when I confess. My past doesn't get the last word. He does."
