The Masculine Mandate
Richard Phillips

Focuses on what biblical manhood actually looks like, starting with God's design for men in Genesis and applying it to things like leadership, marriage, fatherhood, work, friendship, and the church. Probably the most Bible/theology-heavy of the three.

Winning the War in Your Mind
Craig Groeschel

Focuses on the mental and spiritual battles we all deal with — negative thinking, temptation, insecurity, anxiety, destructive habits, etc. The emphasis is on renewing your mind through biblical principles and practical strategies. Probably the most broadly relatable and practical.

Dialed In
Jim Ramos

Focuses specifically on becoming a godly man through 20 characteristics drawn from Scripture. It's very practical and centers on things like leadership, discipline, faithfulness, courage, serving others, and spiritual responsibility. It also has built-in discussion questions/self-assessments, so it seems especially suited for a men's group.
